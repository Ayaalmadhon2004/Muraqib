# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all who participate in Muraqib. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Using welcoming and inclusive language** — avoid assumptions about backgrounds or identities
- **Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences** — we can learn from our differences
- **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism** — feedback helps us all improve
- **Focusing on what is best for the community** — prioritize the collective good
- **Showing empathy towards other community members** — understand that mistakes happen to everyone

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment and bullying** — intimidation, threats, or unwelcome attention
- **Discriminatory language or actions** — based on identity, background, or characteristics
- **Sexual harassment or advances** — unwelcome sexual comments or conduct
- **Personal attacks** — attacking individuals rather than discussing ideas
- **Trolling or deliberately inflammatory posts** — designed to provoke without constructive intent
- **Publishing others' private information** — without explicit permission (doxxing)
- **Other conduct** — which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including:
- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Project repositories
- Any official project communication channels
- Public spaces when representing the project or community

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported to the project maintainers at **aya.alaasel123@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All project leaders are obligated to:
- Respect the privacy and security of the reporter
- Investigate complaints thoroughly
- Respond within a reasonable timeframe
- Take appropriate action based on the investigation

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
1. **Warning** — verbal or written notice of inappropriate behavior
2. **Temporary ban** — temporary exclusion from community participation
3. **Permanent ban** — permanent exclusion from the community

The severity of consequences will be determined by project maintainers based on:
- Nature and severity of the violation
- Pattern of behavior (if applicable)
- Intent of the person involved
- Response to the violation

## Appeals

If you believe action taken against you was unjust, you may appeal to the project maintainers with a written explanation within 30 days.
